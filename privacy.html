Privacy

WorkPilot Tools is designed for browser-based file utilities. Where a tool runs locally, your selected files stay in your browser and are not uploaded by this static site.

The site may load third-party services such as analytics, advertising, fonts, or CDN-hosted libraries. Those providers may process technical data according to their own policies.

Do not upload sensitive files to any tool unless you understand how that specific browser feature or third-party library handles data.
