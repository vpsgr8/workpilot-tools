Is AI image generation really free?

Yes, we use free open-source models. You can generate unlimited images at no cost.

Can I use AI images commercially?

Yes, images are free for commercial use. No attribution required, though appreciated.

How do I write good prompts?

Be specific: 'photorealistic golden retriever puppy, sunset lighting, 8k' works better than 'dog'. Include style, lighting, and details.

Why are some images distorted?

AI is still learning. Complex prompts with multiple subjects or text often produce errors. Try simpler descriptions.
