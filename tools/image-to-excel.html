Image to Excel

OCR photo or screenshot → spreadsheet

What is Image to Excel?

Image to Excel uses OCR (optical character recognition) to read text from a photo, scan, or screenshot and export it as an Excel (.xlsx) file. It is ideal for digitizing printed tables, receipts, invoices, and handwritten notes — without retyping everything manually.

Everything runs in your browser. Your image is not uploaded to our servers.

How to Use Image to Excel

  1. Upload your image — JPG, PNG, or WEBP of a table, document, or receipt
  2. Choose layout — Table mode splits columns; Line mode puts each line in one row
  3. Select language — English, Hindi, or both for mixed documents
  4. Click Convert to Excel — OCR runs locally, then downloads your .xlsx file

Key Features

  • Browser OCR — no account or upload required
  • Table & line modes for structured or plain text
  • English & Hindi language support
  • Instant .xlsx download editable in Excel or Google Sheets

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Image to Excel free?

Yes — completely free with no signup or watermarks on your output file.

How accurate is the OCR?

Accuracy depends on image quality, lighting, and font clarity. Use a sharp, well-lit photo for best results and review the spreadsheet after download.

Is my image private?

Yes. OCR and conversion happen entirely in your browser — files never leave your device.

💡 Pro Tip

For table photos, crop tightly around the table and use good lighting. Table layout mode splits columns on tabs or multiple spaces.
