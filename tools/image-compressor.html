How much can I compress images?

JPGs typically reduce 60-80%, PNGs 40-60%. A 4MB photo often becomes 600KB with minimal quality loss.

Our smart algorithm maintains visual quality. At 70-80% compression, differences are invisible to the human eye.

What's the best format for web?

WEBP offers best compression. JPG for photos, PNG for graphics with transparency.
